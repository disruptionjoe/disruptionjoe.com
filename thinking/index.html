Public repo 01 / formalization lab

Time as Finality

The lead research room asks whether temporal order can be reconstructed from causal access, stable records, and finality. The useful move is the discipline: claims are typed, tested, demoted, absorbed, or killed.

This is how ambitious intuition earns a place on the wall. Not by sounding profound. By surviving contact with executable tests and explicit failure conditions.

Temporal Issuance

This room studies the uncomfortable source question: what, if anything, genuinely crosses a bounded system's interface? It keeps speculative work under stewardship, claim ledgers, absorber maps, and kill criteria.

Public repo 03 / boundary content room

GU Formalization

This is the room where a bold theory gets treated as a generator of precise questions. The work follows assumptions, proof-grade labels, rollback conditions, and failure states instead of defending a conclusion.

The client-facing lesson is direct: hard problems become useful when the room can name the load-bearing claims and attack them without losing the thread.

Public repo 04 / governance design room

Architecture of Legitimacy

This room studies contribution, credit, reward, capture resistance, and governance transition as one coupled design problem. The question is not whether openness sounds good. The question is whether collaboration can become the best move available.
