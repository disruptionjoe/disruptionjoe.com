Entrance / orientation

The rooms are not content buckets.

They are places where Joe's practice, passion, and purpose become inspectable. Walk through them and the pattern is obvious: the method keeps testing itself in public.

Practice room 01 / formalization lab

Time as Finality

Ambitious intuition enters here only if it accepts pressure: typed claims, failure conditions, executable tests, and the willingness to demote a beautiful idea when the structure will not hold.

This room holds the source question without letting it become fog. It asks what crosses a boundary, what only appears to cross, and what has to be killed because the evidence does not deserve the claim.
