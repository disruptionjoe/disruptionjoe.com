About Joe

I help groups make important conversations count.

I design and facilitate focused conversations when the challenge matters, the perspectives differ, or progress has become harder than it should be.

For roughly ten years, I have developed and refined a facilitation playbook informed by Google's Design Sprint, Atlassian's Team Playbook, Liberating Structures, and my experience across technology, product, leadership, and business.

I am not there to take the decision away from the people accountable for it. I create focused, engaging sessions that help the right people surface what they know, examine alternatives and tradeoffs, and make meaningful progress together.

You can begin with a free 30-minute discovery call and one customized session. There is no requirement to start with a major consulting commitment.
