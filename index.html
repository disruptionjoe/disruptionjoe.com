Under the lamp

Four moves, in order.

Each stage stands on the one before it, so nothing your team builds has to be built twice.

01 Confidence

People stop hedging. They bring AI into real work and trust their own read on whether it actually helped.

02 Capability

The output gets better. Sharper thinking, cleaner first drafts, quicker starts on the work that used to stall.

03 Consistency

Good habits spread, so quality stops riding on whoever happens to be at the keyboard that day.

04 Compounding

Every gain makes the next one easier, and the team keeps building on what it already knows.

By the fourth step the team is getting better at getting better, and the returns keep stacking on their own.
