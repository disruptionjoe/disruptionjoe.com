# Disruption Joe Brand Voice Standard

Status: current governing voice check for disruptionjoe.com.

Use this before editing public copy.

## Voice

Disruption Joe should sound like a sharp operator with real room experience, strong taste, and a useful point of view.

The copy should be:

- practical, but not boring
- strategic, but not abstract
- warm, but not soft
- confident, but not inflated
- sales-aware, but not salesy
- opinionated enough to feel authored
- clear enough that a serious buyer understands what Joe does and why it matters

Plain English wins. Concrete stakes win. Specific promises win. Strong rhythm wins.

## Positioning

Disruption Joe is not a prompt-training shop, automation vendor, implementation firm, or generic AI speaker.

The work is AI adoption through designed experiences: sessions, methods, activation systems, and interactive environments that help people change how they think, work, decide, and collaborate with AI.

## Buyer Feeling

The site should make the buyer feel:

1. This person understands the real adoption problem.
2. This is more serious and more useful than another AI workshop.
3. There is a method here.
4. The experience itself will change how our people behave.
5. I know what to do next.

## Avoid

- generic AI consulting language
- "AI transformation" fog
- over-polished SaaS voice
- motivational language without operational teeth
- hype
- filler
- copy that could belong to any AI enablement firm
- "unlock," "empower," "transform," and "future-proof" unless there is a concrete reason
- visible internal labels, curation notes, or implementation language on public pages
- em dashes

## Capability Language

"Capability" is structurally important, but it should not become wallpaper.

Use it where the architecture needs it. Elsewhere, vary the language with:

- better work
- shared practice
- operating progress
- useful judgment
- repeatable improvement
- business leverage
- stronger execution

## Evidence

Evidence copy must point to something inspectable: an artifact, map, trace, screenshot, public example, video, or approved client-safe excerpt.

Do not invent proof. Do not imply private client proof from personal or public operating artifacts. If an evidence asset is not public-safe yet, track that in repo notes, not as public page copy.
