How It Works

Three steps. Ten minutes per team member.

Step 01

You sponsor it.

Drop your name, email, and team size. You get back a link you can forward to your team.

Step 02

Your team responds.

Each team member spends about 10 minutes with the Snapshot. No login, no install, nothing to learn. Their answers are unattributed in the deck you get back.

Step 03

You get the deck.

Within 24 to 48 hours of the last team member finishing, your unattributed sponsor deck lands in your inbox. Yours to keep, no follow-up required.

Privacy and Attribution

What we do not do with your team's responses.

  • No identity captured by the system. The Snapshot does not ask for names, emails, or anything personally identifying from individual team members.
  • Responses are unattributed in sponsor-facing outputs. Even you, as the sponsor, cannot tell which response came from which person on your team.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer team responses. They are not training data for third-party models.
  • The Snapshot is free at delivery. It does not commit you or your team to anything else.

The full information-handling statement lives on the Privacy page. Sponsor contact details (your name and email) are captured so we can send the deck back to you; team responses stay unattributed.
