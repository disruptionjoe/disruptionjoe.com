Privacy and Attribution

Built for honest participation.

  • Sponsors receive team-level patterns, not a "who said what" report.
  • Participant responses are used to create team-level themes and readiness patterns.
  • Participants do not need to enter their names or emails in the response form.
  • Individual responses are not shared back to the sponsor by name or as a respondent-by-respondent report.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer team responses. They are not training data for third-party models.
  • The Snapshot is designed to give you a useful team-level read before you decide whether a deeper activation makes sense.

Common Questions

What happens after I get the deck?
If the Snapshot is useful, the next step is an Activation Planning Session to interpret the patterns and design the right Team AI Activation. No hard commitment.
Why is this free?
Because it is useful even if you stop there, and it helps you decide whether a deeper activation makes sense.
What if my team is uncomfortable with AI?
That is one of the most common findings. Cautious rooms are well served by this; it is built for them as much as for enthusiastic ones.
What does my team actually do?
Each participant opens a private link and is interviewed by an AI: a short, conversational set of questions about how they work with AI today. Most people finish in under 10 minutes. At the end, the AI gives them a block of text to copy and paste into a simple submission form. Once the team has submitted, the AI analyzes all the responses together and returns team-level patterns. Individual answers are never connected back to the people who gave them.
