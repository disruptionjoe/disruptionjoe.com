# Standard elevator, service choice one screen inside

Joe approved restoring the “How I Work with Clients” elevator with the same
vertical/horizontal gestures and doorway styling as the other floors. The
Thinking Better Together and AI Services buttons move to a dedicated screen
immediately left or right of the elevator. Space switching appears inside
an offering, and the choice has its own progress control.

Existing exhibit content and desktop placards are preserved. Asset versions
are updated so returning visitors receive the revised mobile behavior.

Validation: syntax, DOM navigation checks (both directions, choice, switching,
inspector/contact intent, return, repeated selection, active panel isolation),
and desktop service-layout regression checks pass. Rendered layout was not
verified because the earlier browser-preview denial remains in effect.
