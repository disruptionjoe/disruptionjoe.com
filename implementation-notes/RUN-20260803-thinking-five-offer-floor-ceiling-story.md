# RUN-20260803 Five-Offer Floor/Ceiling Story

## Bottom line

Work With Joe now tells one coherent five-display story: see the floor,
establish the floor, raise the floor, strengthen leadership, and extend
high-performance capability.

## Raise the Floor sequence

The sequence begins on the entrance-side west-wall segment and continues from
left to right across the north wall:

1. **Understand where you are**
   - Static: See who is meaningfully using AI, where it is helping, what is
     getting in the way, and where to begin.
   - Dynamic: Maps meaningful usage, use cases, uneven capability, and
     friction; outputs a capability map, use-case map, friction map, and
     grounded starting recommendation.
   - CTA: Understand your starting point.
2. **Build reliable AI ways of working**
   - Static: Help people turn recurring work into reusable, measurable
     AI-supported processes they can build again.
   - Dynamic: Participants improve one real process, test it, learn to build
     another, measure it, and receive follow-up on use and transfer.
   - CTA: Plan a real-work session.
3. **Connect what works and scale it**
   - Static: Connect successful workflows, handoffs, standards, and measures
     so useful capability can spread across teams.
   - Dynamic: Connects practices into a shared system with clear inputs,
     outputs, ownership, review, reuse, and a path from team to organization.
   - CTA: Explore how to scale what works.

## Raise the Ceiling sequence

From the room-center view, the opposite south wall reads left to right:

4. **Help leaders guide AI-enabled change**
   - Static: Understand what is happening, make the decisions that move the
     work forward, and lead with greater clarity.
   - Dynamic: Works through the situation, perspectives, constraints, and
     tradeoffs to produce priorities, decisions, ownership, commitments, and
     useful progress signals.
   - CTA: Discuss leadership advisory.
5. **Push high-value work further**
   - Static: Help capable people use AI and agents to investigate, synthesize,
     decide, and create beyond their current frontier.
   - Dynamic: Uses a bounded engagement to advance an important research,
     strategy, innovation, or specialized knowledge-work challenge and
     strengthen the team's continuing capability.
   - CTA: Discuss an important challenge.
