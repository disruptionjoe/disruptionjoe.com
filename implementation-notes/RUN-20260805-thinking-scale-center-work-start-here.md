# Scale and center Work With Joe Start Here

## Objective

Make the complete desktop Start Here installation about twenty percent smaller
and place its combined neon-and-placard composition at a more natural reading
height.

## Result

- Grouped the neon, plaque face, backing, frame, and local lights as one scene
  installation.
- Applied a uniform `0.8` scale to the installation.
- Centered the full visual bounds, from the plaque frame bottom through the
  neon top, on the Work With Joe room's vertical midpoint at `y=2.65`.
- The neon moves materially lower while the plaque and neon retain their exact
  internal spacing and alignment.

## Preserved

The guide copy and texture, east-wall bay, viewing direction, five product
displays, Soundcheck, Who Is Joe hallway, room geometry, mobile experience,
and all other installations remain unchanged.

## Validation

- JavaScript syntax and repository tests.
- Exact composite scale, center, and calculated visual bounds.
- Guide content and surrounding-placement invariants.
- YAML and repository whitespace checks.
