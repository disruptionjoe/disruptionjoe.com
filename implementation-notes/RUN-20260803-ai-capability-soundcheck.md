# AI Capability Soundcheck

Date: 2026-08-03
Status: Joe-authorized website experience and public GitHub deployment batch

## Public route

`/soundcheck/`

## Experience

The visitor arrives directly behind a DJ booth rather than passing through a
second entry screen or taking a maturity assessment. Twelve knowledge-work
adoption frictions appear as signals in a layered crowd and as channels on the
mixing board. The visitor can:

- bring one or more signals into the mix;
- adjust their relative intensity;
- solo a channel to inspect a paraphrased public signal, work manifestation,
  source links, and Joe's diagnostic lens;
- identify that the organizational floor is stable and the immediate goal is
  to push the frontier;
- play the combined room and receive a directional route into the five
  buyer-facing Work With Joe paths.

The result is explicitly a useful conversation locator, not a maturity score,
assessment verdict, or statistically representative diagnosis.

## Room integration

The Soundcheck appears inside Work With Joe as a separate `Start here`
experience. The social-preview image remains its own wall installation rather
than a regular exhibit display. A small floor outline signals its interaction
zone; approaching it opens a persuasive dynamic placard, and the placard owns
the button that enters the Soundcheck. The wall image does not navigate
directly. It does not become a sixth offer or replace the five buyer-facing
displays.

The live board has a three-step maximum orientation: move a fader, use Solo to
inspect a problem, and select a problem directly in the crowd. Visitors can
skip it, reopen it from `How it works`, or proceed immediately once the three
instructions are complete.

## Evidence posture

The page uses paraphrased signals and readable public source links from the
June through August 3, 2026 research packet. The directional evidence note is
visible in the entry experience. The page does not present the ranking as a
scientific census and does not publish inaccessible research-session citation
tokens.

## Routing posture

The interaction maps the combined signal mix toward:

1. Understand where you are.
2. Build reliable AI ways of working.
3. Connect what works and scale it.
4. Help leaders guide AI-enabled change.
5. Push high-value work further.

The route is a primary next conversation, not a one-to-one claim that each
problem has one offer or method.
