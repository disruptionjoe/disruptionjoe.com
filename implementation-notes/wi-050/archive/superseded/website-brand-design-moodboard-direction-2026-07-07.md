## Working Design Thesis

The site should feel like a premium strategic field manual you can enter.

The strongest direction is not a normal consulting website with expensive-looking images. It is a black-and-tan operating environment: part architectural blueprint, part live facilitation room, part field notebook, part evidence gallery.

The brand should make AI adoption feel physical. Not "AI magic." Not software dashboards. Not thought leadership decoration. The visitor should feel the work taking shape as rooms, paths, thresholds, objects, signals, maps, annotated systems, and useful proof.

## Primary Direction

Name: black-and-tan strategic field architecture.

Use Lane 1 architectural wireframe environments as the site's world.

Use Lane 2 symbolic concept images as the objects, incidents, frictions, and activation moments inside that world.

The final system should feel:

- serious without feeling corporate
- immersive without becoming entertainment for its own sake
- premium without becoming glossy
- strange enough to be memorable
- concrete enough that a buyer understands the work
- operational enough that the experience supports the method

## Mood-Board Read

### Lane 1: Architectural Wireframe Environments

Folder level assets include threshold/corridor, cathedral, museum/gallery, contemporary gallery, and strategic operating room variations.

Observed design value:

- The threshold/corridor images create orientation, forward movement, and "enter the work" energy.
- The cathedral-like space gives height, ceremony, and method-level gravity. Use carefully so it does not become inflated.
- The museum/gallery rooms feel like proof, curation, and inspectable artifacts.
- The strategic operating room image feels closest to buyer work: decisions, systems, facilitation, alignment, and executive seriousness.
- The gallery/workshop/operating-room family is probably more brand-useful than the cathedral family for most pages.

Implementation posture:

- Use these as spatial systems, not decorative hero wallpaper.
- Let architecture organize content: entry, room, table, wall, shelf, corridor, map, chamber.
- Keep linework clean and spacious. Avoid noisy "data room" styling.
- Use motion as drawing, reveal, shift, or navigation through space.

### Lane 2: AI-Native Symbolic Concept Images

Sampled assets include dark paths, glowing doors, scattered spheres, signal voids, permissions gaps, quiet power-user scenes, and activation/object metaphors.

Observed design value:

- `path in the dark.jpg` is good for orientation, readiness, and the next step.
- `hesitation.jpg` is a clear threshold metaphor. It can represent the moment before adoption becomes behavior.
- `permissions gap 1.jpg` turns organizational blockage into a physical gap. Strong for enablement architecture or governance friction.
- `signal void.jpg` is strong as a signal/constraint/activation metaphor. It has a product-design feel.
- `scattered spheres.jpg` is useful for scattered experimentation, variance, and unstructured individual AI use.
- `Quiet power user 2.jpg` shows a central luminous unit activating a field. Strong for power users, patterns, and repeatable practice.
- `quiet power user 1.jpg` is dramatic but closer to cinematic tech imagery. Use sparingly because it risks feeling too "person at screen."

Implementation posture:

- Use Lane 2 as concept objects and proof metaphors, not the whole visual language.
- Avoid using human-at-screen imagery as the main brand move.
- The best symbolic assets are object-based: spheres, cubes, doors, gaps, paths, signals, structures.
- Keep each visual tied to a buyer job. No abstract coolness without a job.

## Design System Ingredients

### Color

Core palette:

- near black
- charcoal black
- warm tan
- camel
- amber
- soft gold light

Use white or bone only for high-legibility text where needed. Avoid blue-neon AI, rainbow gradients, bright SaaS accents, and one-note beige/tan dominance. The page should read as black field plus warm signal, not tan lifestyle brand.

### Typography

Use the existing brand fonts unless later brand review says otherwise.

Typography should feel like:

- field manual headings
- operating notes
- room labels
- annotated diagrams
- short proof captions
- direct-response clarity

Avoid oversized marketing copy inside compact panels. Let big type appear only where the page has true room for it.

### Layout

Preferred structures:

- threshold openings
- annotated rooms
- evidence shelves
- operating tables
- diagnostic maps
- framework diagrams
- field-note margins
- object galleries
- path and decision sequences
- page sections that feel like different spaces in the same operating environment

Avoid:

- generic card grids as the default
- nested cards
- stock SaaS feature sections
- floating dashboards
- decorative orbs
- busy particle fields
- fake readable interface text

### Motion And Interaction

Motion should feel like facilitation, not spectacle.

Use:

- line drawing
- room entry
- threshold reveal
- signal activation
- object focusing
- artifact inspection
- map progression
- subtle parallax tied to spatial movement

Avoid:

- constant background motion
- confetti-like particles
- generic scroll-jacking
- excessive glow
- animation that delays the buyer from understanding the offer

AI Accelerated Thinking is the exception: that page is allowed to have a more immersive entrance if the payoff is meaningful, intentional, and performant within reason.

## Page-Level Visual Mapping

### Home

Role: first entry into the operating environment.

Visual direction:

- Start with threshold / corridor energy.
- Make the buyer feel they are entering a designed system of work.
- Use line/circle/sphere motifs to show scattered use becoming shared practice and business leverage.
- Treat "Evidence, not theater" as an inspectable gallery or artifact wall only if real proof assets are available.
- Prompts may appear as artifacts, but the homepage should center behavior change, better work, useful judgment, and compounding business value.

### Services

Role: help the buyer understand what they can buy and why each offer exists.

Visual direction:

- Use strategic operating room, operating table, or field-manual spread.
- Each service should feel like a distinct intervention mode, not a marketing tile.
- The service set can be shown as a practical system: workshop room, architecture map, thinking studio.

### Method

Role: make the method feel real, repeatable, and authored.

Visual direction:

- Use monumental library, archive, gallery, or method chamber energy.
- Make the stool/triangle/line-circle-sphere logic visible through annotated diagrams.
- The method should feel like something Joe can walk a room through, not a theoretical model.

### AI Activation Playbook

Role: make the playbook feel like a practical activation object.

Visual direction:

- Use workshop room / activation lab / field manual table.
- Treat the playbook as a working object: pages, tabs, maps, annotated scenarios, facilitation moves.
- The page should feel tactile and usable, not like a PDF landing page.

### AI Enablement Architecture

Role: show the structural work behind serious adoption.

Visual direction:

- Use strategic operating room, command center, diagnostic map, or permissions-gap metaphors.
- This page should feel like mapping the organizational conditions that make AI usable.
- Keep it sober, structured, and practical.

### AI Accelerated Thinking

Role: prove the site's experiential ambition.

Visual direction:

- This page must not become stacked webpage sections.
- Practice / Passion / Purpose should be zones, rooms, chambers, exhibits, or explorable spaces.
- It can feel like a video game, studio, lab, strange building, or purposeful exhibit.
- Use Lane 1 as the world and Lane 2 as interactive artifacts, signal objects, thresholds, and discovered moments.
- A slightly longer entrance is acceptable if the user feels the payoff.

### About

Role: build trust in Joe's taste, room experience, and operator judgment.

Visual direction:

- Use gallery, studio wall, evidence shelf, or working notebook structure.
- The About page should feel authored and grounded, not self-mythologizing.
- Avoid making sharp lines feel performed. Preserve the stance: Joe avoids hype, empty performance, and AI theater.

## Image Prompting Guardrails

Lane 1 should be prompted as black-and-tan architectural wireframe environments in a black void, with clean blueprint linework, strong depth, restrained glow, and minimal detail.

Lane 2 should be prompted as AI-native symbolic concept images with one clear metaphor, strong negative space, black/tan/amber palette, premium cinematic restraint, and no generic AI icons.

Do not use:

- robots
- brains
- blue neon
- literal code rain
- generic office teams
- fake dashboards
- cluttered sci-fi rooms
- motivational stock photos
- readable generated text
- logos unless explicitly provided
