## Core Rule

Generate symbolic concept images, not generic AI imagery and not complete website mockups.

Use one clear metaphor from the theme. The image should feel like a designed object, threshold, field, signal, room, or moment that makes an abstract idea physical.

## Prompt Shape

```text
make 5 divergently explorative versions of this:
[Specific visual metaphor for the theme, 1-3 sentences. Make it dimensional, restrained, and concept-first.],
3D rendered, realistic dimensional depth,

[Brand Alignment Snippet]
```

## Brand Alignment Snippet

```text
Brand colors only: near-black field #0A0A0A, white #F5F5F5, signal gold #D4BC94; no other hues. The gold reads as cool warm tan, not amber or orange. Space Grotesk / Space Mono visual weight: modern, precise, confident. Activation field aesthetic: dark professional base, warm gold spotlight or haze, very low-density suspended particles inside the light, sparse thin gold signal traces, tiny meaningful sparks at connection points only. Premium consulting credibility; restrained weird creative AI energy, not decorative. Realistic dimensional depth, matte black surfaces, believable contact shadows. Corporate-buyer safe.

Avoid: serif fonts, neon, cyberpunk, robots, AI brains, circuit boards, blue glow, orange, warm amber, bright yellow, heavy gold luxury branding, dense glitter, fireworks, purple, white background zones.
```

## Thinking Museum Adaptation

For new repo exhibit images, start from the repo's accelerated-thinking boundary:

```text
make 5 divergently explorative versions of this:
A symbolic 3D concept image for [repo name]. The metaphor should ask: [boundary question]. Show [one physical metaphor: threshold, signal field, map, room, object, path, fragment, altar, archive, command surface, or unstable structure]. Keep the scene minimal enough to read as an exhibit object inside a black-and-tan architectural museum.,
3D rendered, realistic dimensional depth,

[Brand Alignment Snippet]
```

## What Made The Earlier Images Work

- One focal metaphor instead of a busy system.
- Strong black negative space.
- Warm tan/gold light used as signal, not decoration.
- Tiny sparks only where a connection, decision, or activation happens.
- Matte dimensional surfaces and believable shadows.
- No generic AI icons, brains, robots, blue glow, code rain, or stock SaaS polish.
