## Master Draft Status

The refined brand-voice draft is strong and should be treated as the best current master draft for the site direction.

It restores the core spine, carries the section logic better than the prior flattened version, and is much closer to the intended Disruption Joe voice.

Before moving toward implementation, preserve the current draft's stronger structure and voice while addressing the risks below.

## 1. Reduce Overuse Of "Capability" In Final Surface Copy

"Capability" is the correct architecture spine and should stay central.

However, in final copy, be careful that the word does not become repetitive or start to feel like wallpaper.

Use "capability" where it matters structurally, but vary surrounding language with phrases like:

- better work
- shared practice
- operating progress
- useful judgment
- repeatable improvement
- business leverage
- stronger execution

The goal is not to weaken the concept. The goal is to keep the copy alive.

## 2. Make AI Accelerated Thinking Unmistakably Non-Standard

The AI Accelerated Thinking page should not become a normal webpage with dressed-up sections.

Implementation instruction:

This is the one page on the site that is allowed to feel like wandering through a video game, studio, lab, or strange purposeful building.

Practice / Passion / Purpose should not simply become stacked content sections. They should feel like zones, chambers, rooms, exhibits, or explorable spaces.

A slightly longer load or entrance moment is acceptable if the payoff feels meaningfully cool and intentional. It still needs to be performant within reason, but this page can prioritize immersion more than the rest of the site.

## 3. Watch The Tone Of "Allergic To Empty AI Theater"

The phrase is good and may be right, but it is slightly spikier than the rest of the About page.

Review whether it feels genuinely like Joe or whether it feels like a writer trying to sound sharp.

If it feels too performed, soften it while preserving the underlying point: Joe avoids hype, empty performance, and AI theater.

## 4. Confirm Proof Assets Before Implementing Home Section 4

The Home "Evidence, not theater" section is architecturally strong, but it depends on real evidence.

Before implementing it, confirm what public-safe assets, screenshots, artifacts, anonymized examples, or concrete traces are available.

The section should not regress into polished claims without inspectable evidence.

If proof assets are not ready yet, mark those slots clearly as needing curation rather than filling them with generic copy.

## 5. Be Careful With "Prompts" On The Homepage

Prompts can appear as part of the visible work, but they should not become the center of the homepage story.

The brand is not prompt-led.

The center is organizational capability, behavior change, enablement architecture, practical judgment, and compounding business value.

Prompts are useful artifacts and tools inside that larger system.

## Bottom Line

This draft is good enough to become the working master direction, but it should still go through an experiential implementation pass before source changes.

The main risks are:

- repeated "capability" language flattening the voice
- AI Accelerated Thinking getting implemented as a normal page
- About getting slightly too sharp or self-stylized
- Evidence sections becoming claims without assets
- prompts accidentally becoming too central
