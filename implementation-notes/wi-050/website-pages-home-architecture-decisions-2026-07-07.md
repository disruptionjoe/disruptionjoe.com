# Homepage Architecture Decisions

Status: architectural decisions and insights for WI-050 fresh website build
Date: 2026-07-07
Page: Home
Work Card: WI-050
Owning implementation repo: `djc-website`

## Site Spine

- Home
- Services
- Method
- AI Activation Playbook
- AI Enablement Architecture
- AI Accelerated Thinking
- About

## Page-Level Purpose

The homepage answers one primary question:

> Am I in the right place?

It should not explain everything Joe does. It should help the right buyers recognize their problem, trust that there is a practical path forward, and decide whether to continue deeper into Services, Method, or Contact.

## Homepage Journey

The homepage should move through six questions:

1. Am I in the right place?
2. Why does this matter now?
3. What does Joe actually do?
4. Can I trust this?
5. Is there something deeper here?
6. How do we start?

Each section should answer one question and naturally create the next.

## Overall Homepage Balance

The homepage should be approximately:

- 60% buyer/problem
- 30% Joe's approach
- 10% deeper philosophy

The page should progressively reveal depth. It should not lead with the deepest identity.

## Global Homepage Posture

The homepage should feel:

- practical
- calm
- intelligent
- buyer-centered
- distinctive
- generous
- confident without being pushy

It should avoid:

- AI hype
- fear-based urgency
- abstract transformation language
- consultant jargon
- research-first positioning
- self-impressed philosophy
- generic AI trainer positioning

## Core Homepage Anchor

The strongest homepage anchor is:

> Your team has AI. Now make it capable.

This line should remain the dominant first-screen idea unless there is a deliberate decision to replace it.

Supporting ideas may include access, confidence, and scattered use, but the main promise should be organizational capability.

## Core Buyer Problem

The homepage should establish that many organizations now have access to AI, but access has not become shared organizational capability.

The buyer should feel:

> Yes, that is us. We have tools, activity, experimentation, and uneven usage, but not yet shared capability or business leverage.

## Buyer Clusters The Homepage Must Serve

### Operators / Executives

CEO, COO, GM, Enterprise Sponsor, Managing Partner.

They care about:

- leverage
- execution
- standards
- measurable progress
- risk
- business value

### People / Learning / Community

People leaders, L&D leaders, Association or Community leaders.

They care about:

- confidence
- readiness
- participation
- psychological safety
- learning transfer
- room design

### Innovation / Depth Buyers

AI Enablement leaders, Research / Innovation leaders, sophisticated founders and executives.

They care about:

- systems
- frontier practice
- scalable methods
- artifacts
- method depth
- proof that Joe thinks differently

## Section 1 - Am I in the Right Place?

### Section Question

> Am I in the right place?

### Section Purpose

Help visitors recognize the problem and recognize themselves.

The section should quickly establish that AI access is no longer the hard part. The hard part is turning access into shared organizational capability.

### Core Message

Your team has AI. Now make it capable.

Or, structurally:

> Your organization has access to AI, but access alone has not created shared capability, better execution, or compounding business value.

### Architectural Decision

This section should start with the buyer's reality, not Joe's method.

It should not start with:

- AI Activation
- Joe's services
- the Method
- the Playbook
- research
- a framework

It should start with the buyer's lived gap.

### Design / Experience Direction

The first screen should be spacious, clear, and buyer-centered.

The visitor should understand the page in seconds.

It should feel like a calm diagnosis, not a pitch.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> This is about performance and leverage, not training.

People / Learning / Community:

> This understands that people are uneven, uncertain, and need support.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> This person sees the deeper adoption pattern, not just tool usage.

### Supporting Insight

The first impression should not be "Joe teaches AI."

It should be:

> Joe helps organizations move from AI access to organizational capability.

### Avoid

- Opening with "AI Activation" as the main concept before the buyer recognizes the problem
- Making the problem only about confidence
- Making the problem only psychological or individual
- Starting with hype, fear, or future-of-work language
- Leading with Joe's terminology before the buyer's reality

## Section 2 - Why Does This Matter Now?

### Section Question

> Why does this matter now?

### Section Purpose

Create urgency without fear.

This section should explain why this is now a business problem, not just a technology or training issue.

### Core Message

Technology is no longer the primary bottleneck. Organizational capability is.

The tools are available. The gap is whether people, teams, and organizations can use them together in ways that improve the business.

### Architectural Decision

This is the first implicit introduction of the Four Cs:

- Confidence
- Capability
- Consistency
- Compounding

They should appear as the natural progression organizations need to develop, not as a branded framework.

### Four Cs Logic

The causal logic is:

> Confidence enables Capability.
> Capability enables Consistency.
> Consistency enables Compounding.

The order matters.

Do not scramble the sequence.

### Design / Experience Direction

The visual concept should be four words with upward momentum.

Compounding should feel like the highest-leverage state.

Possible visual:

- Confidence up
- Capability up up
- Consistency up up up
- Compounding up up up up

The section should feel like rising leverage.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> We are probably leaving value on the table.

People / Learning / Community:

> Our people need confidence and shared ways of working, not pressure.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> The frontier is no longer only technical. Organizational capability is the bottleneck.

### Supporting Insight

This section should not argue that "AI is changing everything."

It should argue that the organizations that benefit will be the ones that can turn AI access into measurable capability.

### Avoid

- Presenting the Four Cs as a clever framework too early
- Fear-based urgency
- "Move fast or die"
- "AI will replace you"
- Making this only about individual confidence
- Losing the causal sequence from confidence to compounding

## Section 3 - What Does Joe Actually Do?

### Section Question

> What does Joe actually do?

### Section Purpose

Explain the solution without becoming a services list.

This section should help the visitor understand Joe's role in moving an organization from scattered usage toward organizational capability.

### Core Message

Joe helps organizations move from one-dimensional AI use to multidimensional capability.

### Architectural Decision

This section introduces AI Activation as the commercial operating concept, not as a workshop or standalone product.

AI Activation is the path for helping people and organizations build the confidence, capability, and consistency that create compounding business impact.

### Key Visual / Conceptual Model

Line -> Circle -> Sphere

#### Line

Scattered individual AI usage.

This can create hidden costs:

- inconsistent quality
- duplicate work
- fragmented practices
- isolated experimentation
- limited measurable value

#### Circle

Coordinated team capability.

Teams begin sharing practices. Standards emerge. Waste decreases. AI starts creating operational improvements.

#### Sphere

Integrated organizational capability.

AI becomes embedded into how the organization works. The company begins creating new business value, better workflows, stronger decisions, and compounding leverage.

### Economic Overlay

Line / Circle / Sphere and Cost / Savings / Business Value are not competing models.

They should work together:

- Line = scattered usage, often hidden cost
- Circle = coordinated capability, often savings
- Sphere = integrated capability, new business value

### Role of Joe's Methods

This section should lightly connect the methods without becoming a services menu:

- AI Activation Playbook accelerates confidence and capability in people and teams.
- AI Enablement Architecture turns those gains into consistency and compounding organizational leverage.

### Design / Experience Direction

Use the Line / Circle / Sphere metaphor visibly.

The section should feel dimensional and memorable.

It should show that growth changes shape, not just volume.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> This is how scattered use becomes business leverage.

People / Learning / Community:

> This still starts with people and confidence.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> This person thinks in systems, not sessions.

### Supporting Insight

The Four Cs explain what grows.

Line / Circle / Sphere explains how growth changes shape.

Together they form one of the homepage's most distinctive ideas.

### Avoid

- Turning this into a service list
- Explaining every offer
- Reducing the final state to generic "business value" without integrated capability
- Losing the Playbook / Enablement relationship
- Making AI Activation sound like a single workshop

## Section 4 - Can I Trust This?

### Section Question

> Can I trust this?

### Section Purpose

Provide evidence rather than unsupported claims.

This section should make visitors think:

> Oh, this is real.

### Core Message

The work produces useful artifacts, practical behavior change, organizational insight, and deeper thinking.

### Architectural Decision

This section should be organized around three kinds of evidence:

1. Evidence from People
2. Evidence from Organizations
3. Evidence from Thinking

### Evidence from People

Should show that Joe can design rooms and experiences that help people actually change behavior.

Possible proof types:

- activation artifacts
- session outputs
- facilitation examples
- room design examples
- participant experience patterns
- anonymized before/after examples

### Evidence from Organizations

Should show that the work creates organizational clarity, not just good sessions.

Possible proof types:

- enablement maps
- adoption signals
- standards examples
- capability outputs
- workflow or decision artifacts
- anonymized organizational patterns

### Evidence from Thinking

Should show that Joe's methods are grounded in ongoing exploration, systems thinking, and practical frontier work.

Possible proof types:

- AI Accelerated Thinking
- GitHub / public artifacts
- research translated into practical methods
- examples of systems built or pressure-tested

### Design / Experience Direction

Three evidence lanes.

Each lane should contain something concrete, not just claims.

The section should feel like evidence on a table, not a brag reel.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> This produces something usable.

People / Learning / Community:

> This can work with real humans in mixed-readiness rooms.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> This person builds tools, systems, and artifacts, not just opinions.

### Supporting Insight

We deliberately preferred "evidence" over "proof."

Proof can sound defensive.

Evidence says:

> Here is how I think. Here is what the work produces. Judge for yourself.

### Avoid

- Empty claims
- Logo-wall substitution
- Testimonials as the only trust mechanism
- Evidence from Thinking carrying the whole proof burden
- Placeholder proof that sounds invented
- Making this about Joe being smart instead of practically useful

## Section 5 - Is There Something Deeper Here?

### Section Question

> Is there something deeper here?

### Section Purpose

Create conviction, not trust.

Trust means:

> I think Joe can help.

Conviction means:

> I think Joe sees something other people do not.

This section should open the door to that deeper realization.

### Core Message

There is a coherent operating model behind the work.

Joe's methods come from continual exploration, systems thinking, experimentation, public artifacts, and practical work with people and organizations.

### Architectural Decision

This section should reveal depth after practical trust has been established.

It should not lead with research or explain the full philosophy.

It should point toward:

- Method
- AI Accelerated Thinking
- About

### Design / Experience Direction

This should feel like opening a door.

It should be atmospheric, restrained, and intriguing.

It should not be a long explanation.

It should invite the right visitor to go deeper.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> Good. There is more behind this if we need it.

People / Learning / Community:

> This is thoughtful, not tech-bro AI pressure.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> Finally. This is where I should click deeper.

### Supporting Insight

This section exists because deeper identity should be progressively revealed.

The homepage should not lead with Joe's deepest identity, but it should make that depth discoverable.

### Avoid

- Research dump
- Philosophy dump
- Explaining the whole operating system
- Making the section about how smart Joe is
- Making depth compete with commercial clarity
- Routing too aggressively into research before practical trust exists

## Section 6 - How Do We Start?

### Section Question

> How do we start?

### Section Purpose

Reduce uncertainty around beginning the relationship.

This section should make the first step feel useful, calm, and clarifying.

### Core Message

You do not need to know exactly what you need before reaching out.

The first step is an AI Activation Planning Call that helps determine the right path based on the organization, current usage, priorities, and readiness.

### Architectural Decision

The homepage should end with confidence, not urgency.

The final CTA should feel like:

> You have seen the problem.
> You have seen the approach.
> You have seen the evidence.
> If this feels like the right fit, let's talk about your organization.

### Primary CTA

Book an AI Activation Planning Call.

### Secondary CTA

Optional secondary paths may point to:

- Services
- Method
- See how AI Activation works

But the primary action remains the planning call.

### Design / Experience Direction

Calm close.

Minimal new information.

No new methodology.

No pressure.

The section should feel like a confident invitation, not a sales push.

### What It Should Make Buyers Feel

Operators / Executives:

> I do not have to figure out the solution before I call.

People / Learning / Community:

> We can shape the right experience for our audience.

Innovation / Depth Buyers:

> This can become a serious design conversation, not a canned offering.

### Supporting Insight

Most consulting websites end with pressure.

This homepage should end with orientation and confidence.

### Avoid

- "Book now" urgency
- Generic contact language
- Making the call feel like a sales trap
- Adding new content at the end
- Making buyers diagnose themselves before reaching out

## Homepage-Level Supporting Insights

### The Page Is a Progressive Argument

The homepage should not feel like separate content blocks.

It should feel like a sequence:

1. Recognize the problem.
2. Understand why it matters.
3. See the operating model.
4. See evidence.
5. Sense deeper conviction.
6. Begin calmly.

Each section should answer the objection created by the previous section.

### Capability Is the Spine

The homepage must consistently frame the business around capability.

AI is the urgent context.

The value is:

- more capable people
- better team coordination
- measurable organizational progress
- compounding business leverage

### AI Activation Is the Mechanism, Not the First Thought

AI Activation should appear as the way Joe helps.

It should not be the first buyer-facing concept before the buyer has recognized the problem.

### Buyer Outcomes Before Joe's Machinery

Visitors buy outcomes first:

- confidence
- capability
- consistency
- compounding leverage

Then they discover Joe's machinery:

- AI Activation
- AI Activation Playbook
- AI Enablement Architecture
- AI Accelerated Thinking
- facilitation expertise
- systems thinking

### Depth Should Be Revealed Progressively

The homepage should hint at the depth but not carry it.

The deeper work should live in:

- Method
- AI Accelerated Thinking
- About

### Evidence Should Be Concrete

Evidence should show:

- what happens in rooms
- what organizations learn
- what artifacts exist
- how Joe thinks
- why the work is practical

Avoid unsupported proof claims.

### The Homepage Should Not Become

- a methodology overview
- a product catalog
- a research page
- a biography
- an AI hype page
- a generic consultant homepage

### The Homepage Should Feel Like

- the opening chapter of a coherent operating model
- a clear diagnosis of a real buyer problem
- a calm argument for capability
- a doorway into deeper method
- a confident invitation to talk
