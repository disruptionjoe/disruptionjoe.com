# Desktop service-choice entrance

Joe approved the room structure, the three facilitation exhibits, delegated
copy, and live publication through the existing GitHub deployment integration
on 2026-09-09. No hosting migration or mobile redesign is included.

## Delivered scope

- Work With Joe is a two-door hallway with a divided center placard and two
  opinionated side statements.
- AI Activation Services retains the existing five exhibits and room features.
- Thinking Better Together has three inspectable situation exhibits and
  contextual planning links: direction, tradeoffs, cross-group agreement.
- Both service paths lead to Methods and Tools. Who Is Joe and its gallery and
  elevator connect to Methods. The Control supporting hallway connects to Who
  Is Joe; it is no longer a shortcut out of the service-selection experience.
- Existing source elevator and other orientation/research entrances remain.
- Existing artwork and brand components are reused. No client identity,
  private artifact or new quantitative proof claim has been added.
- Phone story initialization and content precede desktop-only additions and
  are preserved; the guided fallback is unchanged.

## Verification and walkthrough boundary

executes production room coordinates and wall builders to check five journeys,
closed obsolete passages and relocated elevator coordinates without a browser.

These checks do not establish visual acceptance, GPU rendering quality or
conversion improvement. Joe's desktop walkthrough is the next acceptance
step, followed by separately scoped mobile iteration. No scheduled reminder
