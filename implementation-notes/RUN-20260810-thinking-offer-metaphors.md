# Clarify Work With Joe offer metaphors

## Objective

Make each of the five primary Work With Joe artworks communicate its specific
stage more clearly while preserving the established display family, and make
displays four and five read as a better-spaced pair.

## Result

- Preserved the shared code-generated artwork system rather than introducing
  five unrelated raster assets or additional runtime loading.
- Rebuilt each central diagram around a stage-specific metaphor:
  - `01 / Identify`: a survey scope reading uneven capability terrain and
    fixing a clear starting level;
  - `02 / Establish`: a loose experimental path entering a closed workflow
    loop and producing repeatable outputs;
  - `03 / Raise`: isolated practices connecting through a strong shared
    capability backbone and spreading across teams;
  - `04 / Lead`: divergent perspectives converging at a decision instrument
    and leaving as aligned ownership lanes;
  - `05 / Extend`: competing paths passing through an evaluation aperture and
    sending a probe beyond the known frontier.
- Kept the existing black, tan, cream, and gold palette, architectural grid,
  nested frame, glow, node-and-line vocabulary, artwork codes, and typography.
- Moved displays four and five inward as a centered pair:
  - center distance reduced from `4.108` to `3.3` scene units;
  - both gained more clearance from the adjacent wall/door boundaries;
  - display scale remains `0.72`, matching the rest of the room.
- Cache-busted the Thinking Museum script.

## Preserved

All five offers, numbers, copy, order, CTAs, dynamic placards, interaction
ranges, display dimensions, wall assignments, neon signs, room geometry,
demand-driven renderer, and mobile experience remain unchanged.

## Validation

- Fresh local desktop walkthroughs from three positions inside Work With Joe:
  - stages four and five read as one balanced Raise the Ceiling pair;
  - stages two and three remain balanced beneath Raise the Floor;
  - stage one remains centered and clear beside Who Is Joe.
- Confirmed all five metaphors remain recognizable at their actual wall scale.
- Confirmed the final entrance camera was restored to `0.00,-8.80` with look
  `3.142,0.000` after the temporary walkthrough views.
- JavaScript syntax, repository tests, semantic invariants, YAML, cache
  reference, and whitespace checks pass.
