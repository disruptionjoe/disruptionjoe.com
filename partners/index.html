How It Works

Your firm hosts the relationship. Joe runs the hour.

Your clients get a 60-minute session designed to feel useful no matter where they are starting from. The format borrows from the regular AI sessions Joe already runs, framed for your audience and their work.

You Do

Joe Does

Run the working hour.

Joe runs the 60 minutes for your audience: how to get started with AI, how to do it smarter if they are already in motion, why most adoption and transformation efforts stall, and what their honest next move looks like.

Plain trade, named upfront.

Your firm keeps the recording. Use it however you want with your clientele: send it to people who could not attend, embed it in a follow-up, hold it for internal reference.

Repurposing rights are non-negotiable on this offer. If your firm cannot agree to that, the offer is not the right fit and Joe will say so on the discovery call. The reason for naming it up front is that the trade has to be clean for both sides.

Who This Is For

Firms whose clients are AI-curious but not yet sure where to start.

This works best when your client base has people across very different levels of AI familiarity. Some have not started. Some are using a tool or two but unsure if they are doing it well. Some are deeper in but stuck on adoption, buy-in, or what to actually pilot. The session is designed to land for all three.

The first audience is boutique law firms specifically. The shape generalizes to small and mid-size professional services firms more broadly, but the language and examples lead with what works for legal teams thinking about AI in real client work.

If you are the marketing or business development partner at a firm and you are looking for something genuinely useful to bring your clients, this is meant for you.
