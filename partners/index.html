The Trade

Free for qualifying partners. The trade is non-monetary.

The Partner-Hosted AI Activation Session is free for qualifying partners right now. Joe shows up at no charge. The trade is what each side brings: your organization brings the audience and the relationship; Joe brings the tested session and the facilitation.

What your organization is protected from: the session is yours to use with your audience. Your organization keeps the recording, your branding is on the invitation and the introduction, your relationships stay yours, and any conversations that follow are yours to manage. Joe is not pitching your people during the session and is not pulling them into a separate sales funnel. Attendees who want to take an AI next step are pointed at the free AI Readiness Snapshot they can run with their own team.

Content repurposing: Joe retains the right to use clips, snippets, and content extractions from the session in his own channels later. This is named up front so it does not surprise you afterward. If your organization needs different terms (for example, no public clips, or named-attribution constraints), say so on the partner discovery call and Joe will adjust the terms or pass on the run.

The reason this is free right now: Joe is testing partner-hosted sessions as a demand-generation format. Your audience is helping prove whether the format works. Custom or differently-shaped sessions are a separate paid engagement.

Who This Is For

Organizations whose people are AI-curious but not sure where to start.

This works best when your audience has people across very different levels of AI familiarity. Some have not started. Some are using a tool or two but unsure if they are doing it well. Some are deeper in but stuck on adoption, buy-in, or what to actually pilot. The session is designed to land for all three.

The Partner-Hosted AI Activation Session runs across many kinds of partner organizations: professional and industry associations, chambers of commerce, nonprofits, business communities, startup and founder groups, and client-facing service firms. Anywhere a group convenes people who would benefit from a practical, hands-on AI session. The first runs are with boutique law firms, and the shape generalizes well beyond them. Specific session formats rotate over time as we learn which ones produce the strongest demand signal; the partner relationship and the funnel-into-Snapshot mechanic stay the same.

If you lead marketing, membership, programming, or business development for an organization like that, and you are looking for something genuinely useful to bring the people you serve, this is meant for you.
