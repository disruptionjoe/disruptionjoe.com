Why This Exists

Most teams do not have a tool problem.

They have an alignment problem: different comfort levels, hidden concerns, uneven experiments, unclear use cases, and no shared language for what comes next.

Excited

"I can see five things we should try."

Useful energy, but often scattered before the team has a shared frame.

Skeptical

"I do not trust the output yet."

Not resistance to erase. It is real adoption signal.

Overloaded

"I do not have room for another tool."

A practical constraint that should shape the next move.

Quietly using it

"I have a workflow, but no one else knows."

Often the hidden proof that adoption is already starting.
