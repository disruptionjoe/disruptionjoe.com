About Joe

The work has a person behind it.

The rooms, methods, architectures, and artifacts on this site come from a particular way of working: practical, curious, rigorous, human-centered, and unwilling to dress hype up as usefulness.

I build environments where people can think, decide, coordinate, and execute better with AI. Not prompts for their own sake. Not novelty for its own sake. Useful work, built with people in the room.
