The session, reading back

Every session quietly maps your organization.

While your people are busy getting better, the same hour reports back to you. You see where confidence runs thin, where the workflow snags, and which early wins are ready to grow across more teams. You leave with real signal, not an attendance sheet.

Where confidence runs thin Who hangs back, on which tasks, and what would move them forward.
Where the workflow snags The handoffs and half-standards that quietly slow the good ideas down.
Which wins are ready to grow The patterns worth carrying from one team to the next, on purpose.

A working session underway: people learning by doing, and an organization getting easier to read.
