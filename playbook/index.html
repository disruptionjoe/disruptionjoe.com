AI Activation Playbook / current surface

A good AI session can still fail on Monday.

People can leave impressed and go right back to old defaults. Nothing changed if they did not practice on real work, compare with peers, build judgment, and carry something useful back into the organization.

The Playbook exists because behavior change is not delivered through information. It is designed. The full interactive Playbook experience is still being built; this surface holds the current direction.

The threshold is not decoration. It is the handoff from room energy to durable work.

The Playbook designs the conditions for new behavior.

The room is not improvised. It is built so people can try the next version of how they work, safely enough to practice and concretely enough to keep going.

The room tells the truth.

A well-designed activation room does two jobs. It changes participants. It reveals the organization.

You can see where confidence is thin, where workflows snag, where judgment is uncertain, where standards are missing, where managers are not ready, and where the strategy is outrunning behavior.
