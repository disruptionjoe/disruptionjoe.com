The Conversation Playbook

I've spent the past decade exploring, testing, and refining facilitation techniques to understand what actually makes conversations productive. Here are a few examples from my playbook.

These are examples of conversations I can design, not standardized service packages or guaranteed one-hour deliverables.

Figure It Out

Understand what's happening, uncover the real problem, and identify opportunities.

01 / Figure It Out

The Friction Hunt

Identify what's slowing down a team, process, or initiative.

Possible outcomeA shared map of obstacles and where to intervene first.

02 / Figure It Out

The Problem Framing Session

Get everyone aligned on the problem worth solving.

Possible outcomeA clear problem definition and better question to address.

03 / Figure It Out

The Reality Check

Bring evidence and different perspectives into one picture.

Possible outcomeA shared understanding of the situation and its unknowns.

04 / Figure It Out

The Opportunity Map

Explore strategic opportunities, including new technologies and ways of working.

Possible outcomeA prioritized set of opportunities worth testing.

05 / Figure It Out

The AI Starting Line

Figure out where and how your organization should begin using AI.

Possible outcomePractical first experiments and a clearer direction.

Get Organized

Turn ambition and good ideas into focus, structure, and shared direction.

06 / Get Organized

The Priority Reset

Determine what really deserves attention and what can wait.

Possible outcomeAgreed priorities and explicit tradeoffs.

07 / Get Organized

The OKR Workshop

Translate ambitions into concrete goals and measures of success.

Possible outcomeShared objectives and draft key results.

08 / Get Organized

The Roadmap Jam

Organize initiatives, dependencies, and next steps into a workable path.

Possible outcomeA practical roadmap and immediate next moves.

09 / Get Organized

The AI Value Bridge

Connect potential AI use cases to outcomes that matter to the business.

Possible outcomePrioritized use cases with expected value and validation needs.

Make the Move

Reach decisions, work through disagreement, and turn learning into action.

10 / Make the Move

The Lightning Decision Jam

Quickly identify obstacles, develop responses, and choose what to do.

Possible outcomePrioritized problems, promising solutions, and agreed actions.

11 / Make the Move

The Solution Showdown

Compare competing solutions against meaningful criteria.

Possible outcomeClear tradeoffs and a defensible choice.

12 / Make the Move

The Assumption Test

Challenge a promising direction before committing major resources.

Possible outcomeExposed risks and a plan to test critical assumptions.

13 / Make the Move

The Alignment Session

Work through competing perspectives and stakeholder interests.

Possible outcomeClearer agreements, commitments, and unresolved issues.

14 / Make the Move

The Blameless Retro

Learn from what happened without turning it into a blame exercise.

Possible outcomeShared lessons and concrete improvements.
