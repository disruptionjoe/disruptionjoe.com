How I think

The first question is not which exercise to run.

It is which conversation can make the biggest difference. These examples show how changing the conversation changed what became possible.

Startup growth

Changing the growth question

Situation: A startup team was deciding where to invest more in marketing.

Complication: We were debating how to attract more users without understanding what happened to the people already arriving.

My approach: I had the team map the customer journey, estimate conversion through each step, and compare its assumptions with the actual data.

Result: Onboarding emerged as the more immediate problem, and the team could compare acquisition, retention, referral, and reactivation ideas against the same goal.

Decentralized governance

Finding the decisions inside the debate

Situation: Independent stakeholders with different interests needed to move from prolonged debate toward funding programs.

Complication: Broad agreement and the genuinely contentious decisions were tangled together.

My approach: I helped structure the work around what people already agreed on, which decisions still needed to be made, and the sequence that could move proposals forward.

Result: The group had a clearer path from discussion to proposals and funded action.

AI transformation

Defining success before designing the work

Situation: Two groups were supporting the same AI adoption initiative.

Complication: Learning and Development wanted an effective learning experience. The enablement team wanted changes in how people actually worked.

My approach: I surfaced those different definitions of success and used them to shape an approach that could serve both.

Result: The initiative could be designed around learning and practical changes in work, rather than treating either objective as an afterthought.

How it starts

One productive hour at a time.

One hour is the starting point, not a promise that every challenge fits inside sixty minutes.

02

One-hour facilitated working session

I prepare a focused session for the people and challenge, then facilitate the group toward a decision, shared understanding, or meaningful next move.

03

Continue when it is useful

Stop after the first hour, book another focused session, or expand into broader consulting work when the challenge genuinely calls for it.
