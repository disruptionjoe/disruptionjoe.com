Privacy

What we collect, what we do not, and how it is handled.

Last updated: 2026-05-13

This page is a plain-language statement of how disruptionjoe.com handles information. It is a stub. A fuller information-handling page will follow as the business grows. If something here is unclear, email joe@disruptionjoe.com.

What this site collects

We collect anonymous traffic analytics through Google Analytics (page views, referrers, approximate location at the city level, device type). We collect what you submit when you fill in a form on this site (your name, your email, and any details you choose to include).

How the AI Readiness Snapshot handles participant responses

The Snapshot is a free team readiness experience offered on this site. Participants do not need to enter their names or emails in the response form. Participant responses are used to create sponsor-facing team themes and readiness patterns. Individual responses are not shared back to the sponsor by name or as a respondent-by-respondent report. The sponsor's own contact information is captured because Joe needs a way to reply and set up the Snapshot.

How responses are stored

Sponsor contact details and team responses are stored in tools used to run the business (email, a spreadsheet, and the deck-building workflow). Team responses are retained for as long as needed to build and deliver the sponsor deck, plus a short additional window to support follow-up questions. Sponsor contact details are retained for ongoing correspondence; you can ask us to remove yours at any time.

What we do not do with team responses

  • We do not share, sell, or transfer team responses to third parties.
  • We do not use team responses as training data for third-party AI models.
  • We do not turn team responses into a sponsor-facing "who said what" report.

Contact

For any privacy question, including requests to remove your information, email joe@disruptionjoe.com. We respond within a few business days.
