Orientation / why three tracks exist

One adoption problem. Three connected environments.

AI work breaks in different places. Sometimes people need a room where behavior changes. Sometimes leaders need an architecture that makes progress visible. Sometimes the work needs frontier thinking translated back into usable practice.

Choose this track when people need more than an explanation. The Playbook is for designed sessions, field-tested exercises, artifacts, and room dynamics that help people practice AI on work that actually matters.
